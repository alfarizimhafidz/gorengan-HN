## Brand & Style
This design system crafts an upscale culinary presence for modern Indonesian street gourmet, reimagining humble street fritters (*gorengan*) into an artisanal, hygienic, and premium culinary ritual. The brand identity fuses vibrant street warmth with the minimalist polish of modern high-end hospitality. 

The aesthetic is Tactile Contemporary Warmth: bright golden tones evoking golden-brown crispness, appetizing coral accents that suggest chili pairing and heat, balanced by grounding dark espresso neutrals and airy cream backdrops. The emotional resonance is comfort, indulgence, cleanliness, and culinary pride. Generous white space prevents the brand from feeling cluttered, while tactile card surfaces and organic curvature communicate handcrafted quality and approachability.

## Colors
The palette captures the sensory warmth of fresh, artisan frying without greasy visual heaviness.

- **Primary (`#FFCB56`)**: The signature golden-fry hue. Used for core highlights, energetic brand badges, primary focus anchors, and prominent CTAs.
- **Secondary (`#FFA259`)**: Crispy caramelized orange. Drives secondary interactive states, secondary buttons, warm icons, and energetic section accents.
- **Tertiary (`#FF7E7E`)**: Sambal coral-chili. Reserved for high-urgency notifications, hot badge tags (e.g., spicy indicators, chef’s specials), and culinary delight callouts.
- **Neutral (`#2D2319`)**: Rich roast espresso/deep cacao. Serves as the primary text color and structural baseline, providing crisp legibility far superior to stark black.
- **Named Surfaces**:
  - `surface-cream` (`#FFFDF9`): Base canvas tone ensuring high brightness with warm, milky undertones.
  - `surface-custard` (`#FFEDB9`): Subtly saturated container backing for hero modules, quote strips, and distinct menu groupings.
  - `neutral-muted` (`#423223`) and `neutral-subtle` (`#7A6451`): Secondary content, captions, metadata, and breadcrumbs.
  - `border-warm` (`#EED8A1`): Delicate structural borders harmonizing with the cream background.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across all typographic roles to blend Indonesian geometric warmth with high-legibility hospitality modernism. 

Display and headline styles employ tighter letter spacing and assertive weights (`700` and `800`) to create distinct culinary impact without appearing industrial. Body text remains open, relaxed, and set in roast espresso (`#2D2319`) or muted coffee (`#423223`), ensuring high contrast against light surfaces. Small labels, spice tags, and provenance markers leverage all-caps tracking (`label-caps`) to deliver editorial flair reminiscent of contemporary gourmet magazines.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adopts a flexible 12-column grid anchored inside a maximum container width of 1280px, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column stack on mobile. 

Generous macro vertical rhythms (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) isolate menu tiers, catering portfolios, and sourcing stories, preventing the visual crowding common in quick-service restaurant pages. Element grouping inside food cards and informational components follows an 8pt base grid (`space-xs` through `space-lg`), giving snacks, descriptions, and dietary badges clear spatial breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth
Visual hierarchy avoids harsh grey drop-shadows, using instead warm, golden-brown ambient glows that complement fried textures:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#FFFDF9` surface with occasional soft framing using `#FFEDB9`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting)**: Layered with a 1px solid border in `#EED8A1` combined with an ambient glow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(66, 50, 35, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Elevated Trays)**: Used for active snack cards, order trays, and dropdowns: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(66, 50, 35, 0.10), 0px 2px 6px 0px rgba(255, 203, 86, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Fixed Action Bars)**: `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(45, 35, 25, 0.18)`.

Subtle glassmorphic overlays (`background: rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) are applied strictly to sticky navigation bars to let vibrant food photography roll smoothly underneath.

## Shapes
The design embraces rounded, appetizing geometry (`roundedness: 2`). Standard modules and interactive elements carry an 8px (`0.5rem`) base radius, expanding to 16px (`1rem`) on food showcase cards and catering packages, and 24px (`1.5rem`) on hero feature badges and promo callout banners. 

Chips, dietary tags, spice meters, and primary call-to-action pills use full pill-rounding (`9999px`) to maintain a friendly, handheld snack personality.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#FFCB56` background, `#2D2319` bold typography, pill-shaped (`9999px`), 14px vertical by 28px horizontal padding. Subtle hover lift with secondary glow (`#FFA259` border or shadow bloom).
- **Secondary**: Transparent background, 2px border in `#2D2319` or `#FFA259`, text matching the border color. On hover, fills with `#FFEDB9`.
- **Tertiary / Spicy Accent**: Solid `#FF7E7E` with white text for exclusive limited batches or urgent catering inquiries.

### Badges & Chips
- Designed for snack attributes (e.g., "Crispy Bakwan", "Tahu Jeletot Extra Pedas", "Gluten Conscious").
- Compact pill silhouettes with light backgrounds: `#FFEDB9` with `#423223` text, or tinted `#FF7E7E` with deep chili-red text for spicy tiers.
- Border: 1px subtle matching border tint.

### Snack Cards & Product Showcase
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` or `#FFFDF9` resting on `#EED8A1` 1px border.
- Radius: `1rem` (rounded-lg).
- Visual header: High-resolution imagery with an aspect ratio of 4:3 or 1:1, nestled edge-to-edge or with 8px internal card inset.
- Content zone: Generous `1.25rem` padding featuring title (`headline-sm`), tasting notes (`body-sm` in `#7A6451`), price tag in high-contrast espresso, and pill counter/add button.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs**: Rounded-md (`0.5rem`) border in `#EED8A1` on `#FFFDF9`. Focus state invokes a crisp `#FFA259` 2px ring with a soft ambient gold shadow. Label rests cleanly above in `label-md`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Tinted `#FFCB56` fill when checked with `#2D2319` checkmark icon; 2px outline in `#423223` when inactive.

### Narrative & Story Banners
- Sourcing & hygiene assurance blocks: Built with soft `#FFEDB9` background fills, warm charcoal typography, and custom hand-drawn icon badges in deep chili or golden tones.
